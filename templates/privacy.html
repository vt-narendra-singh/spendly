Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Spendly personal expense tracking application (the "Service").

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the expense data you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. Basic usage information, such as login timestamps, may also be recorded to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Service — for example, to display your expense history, generate spending summaries, and secure your account. We do not use your personal expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing practices. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and infrastructure. These providers only process data as necessary to deliver their services to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the Service.
